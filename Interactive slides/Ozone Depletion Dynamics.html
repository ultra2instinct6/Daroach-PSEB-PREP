Ozone Depletion Dynamics (ਓਜ਼ੋਨ ਪਰਤ ਦਾ ਨੁਕਸਾਨ)

The Stratospheric Ozone layer is a protective shield. However, synthetic catalysts break it down, allowing hazardous UV-B rays to reach the surface.

🚨 Board Exam Tip

In 1987, the United Nations drafted the Montreal Protocol to globally freeze the production of CFCs. ੧੯੮੭ ਵਿੱਚ, UNEP ਨੇ ਸੀ.ਐਫ.ਸੀ. ਦੇ ਉਤਪਾਦਨ ਨੂੰ ਰੋਕਣ ਲਈ ਮਾਂਟਰੀਅਲ ਪ੍ਰੋਟੋਕੋਲ ਬਣਾਇਆ।

O2 → (UV Light) → O + O   |   O + O2 → O3
Natural Cycle: High-energy UV radiation splits molecular oxygen. The free atoms bond to form Ozone, which harmlessly absorbs the most dangerous UV-C rays.
CCl2F2 (CFCs) rise untouched
The Pollutants: Chlorofluorocarbons are highly stable in the troposphere. They slowly drift up to the stratosphere over years without breaking down.
Cl• + O3 → ClO + O2
Catalysis: UV light strips a free Chlorine radical from the CFC. One chlorine atom acts as a catalyst, destroying up to 100,000 ozone molecules.
