Periodic Classification: The Zig-Zag Line (ਆਵਰਤੀ ਵਰਗੀਕਰਨ)

In the Modern Periodic Table, a distinct zig-zag line acts as a physical boundary separating metals from non-metals. The elements on this line exhibit intermediate properties.

Board Exam Tip: Nature of Oxides

A highly tested concept: Metallic oxides are generally basic in nature, whereas non-metallic oxides are acidic. Metalloids often form amphoteric oxides (behaving as both).

ਧਾਤਾਂ ਦੇ ਆਕਸਾਈਡ ਖਾਰੀ (basic) ਹੁੰਦੇ ਹਨ, ਅਤੇ ਗੈਰ-ਧਾਤਾਂ ਦੇ ਆਕਸਾਈਡ ਤੇਜ਼ਾਬੀ (acidic) ਹੁੰਦੇ ਹਨ।

Left Side of the Line
Chemical Properties
  • Highly electropositive (form cations).

Physical Properties
  • Malleable (sheets) and ductile (wires).
  • Good conductors of heat and electricity.
  • Solid at room temperature (except Mercury).
The Structural Division
The Zig-Zag Line (ਜ਼ਿਗ-ਜ਼ੈਗ ਲਾਈਨ): As we move from left to right across a period, the metallic character strictly decreases while the non-metallic character increases.

This physical zig-zag border in the p-block visually represents the exact transition point where elements shift from being electron-donors (metals) to electron-acceptors (non-metals).
Borderline Elements
The 7 Core Metalloids
  • Boron (B), Silicon (Si), Germanium (Ge)
  • Arsenic (As), Antimony (Sb)
  • Tellurium (Te), Polonium (Po)
Key Characteristics
  • Exhibit properties of both metals & non-metals.
  • Act as vital semi-conductors.
  • Crucial for electronics (e.g., Silicon chips).
Right Side of the Line
Chemical Properties
  • Highly electronegative (form anions).
  • Tendency to gain electrons to complete octet.
  • Valency is typically 4, 5, 6, or 7.
  • Form Acidic Oxides.
Physical Properties
  • Usually gases or brittle solids at room temp.
  • Poor conductors (insulators).
  • Non-lustrous and non-sonorous.
