Electrode Reactions (ਇਲੈਕਟ੍ਰੋਡ ਪ੍ਰਤੀਕਰਮ)

Solid Cu(s)
Cu2+
2e-
Oxidation at Anode (ਆਕਸੀਕਰਨ)

Solid copper loses electrons up the wire and dissolves into the solution.

Cu(s) Cu2+(aq) + 2e-
Reduction at Cathode (ਲਘੂਕਰਨ)

Copper ions gain electrons from the wire and turn into solid metal.

Cu2+(aq) + 2e- Cu(s)
Exam Tip: Remember OIL RIG.
Oxidation Is Loss (of electrons at Anode).
Reduction Is Gain (of electrons at Cathode).
